# Country pack: France

A country pack is a filtered lens on one country, not a pile of that country's data. Every entry in this pack passes the same relevance test as the registry itself: it can surface a free tier, a trial, free credit, a giveaway code, or a community signal worth acting on, monitoring, and claiming. Technically, the runner validates this pack with the same id and format rules as the registry before running it.

The local pattern to know first: in France, worthwhile trials currently come from small hosting providers (hébergeurs), and they announce them on their own sites and in their Discord servers, not on forums that carry feeds. There is no French equivalent of a LINUX DO-class freebie board with a machine feed. The strongest marker of a claimable trial is the phrase "sans CB" (no bank card).

## Local search terms (verified on live French sources)

- Core nouns: "VPS gratuit", "hébergement gratuit", "serveur gratuit", "essai gratuit"
- No-card markers: "sans CB", "sans carte bancaire" (the highest-precision pair for an owner's safety filter)
- Credit hunting: "crédit offert", "crédit de X $"
- Community announcement phrases: "offres gratuites", "100 % gratuit"
- Umbrella terms, only useful combined with technical nouns: "bon plan", "code promo"
- Negative marker: "échantillons gratuits" means consumer product samples; a source dominated by this term is retail class.

Strongest term formula: (gratuit) combined with (VPS, hébergement, serveur), combined with (sans CB, sans carte bancaire).

## Machine feeds (verified live, both firehose)

- **Korben** (https://korben.info/feed): France's largest tech discovery blog in the web tools and services niche. Of its 23 most recent items, 8 mention the word "gratuit" in the article body; its coverage of free tools and SaaS is routine. Firehose: read on wake, never a trigger.
- **Sebsauvage, Liens en vrac** (https://sebsauvage.net/links/?do=rss): a link blog run by a single human curator for decades. Its density is low (about 4 explicit free-service signals out of 50 items) but its curation quality is high and sometimes exactly on category, for example free DDNS services. Firehose.

## Agent-leg hunting grounds

- **DISBOARD, Discord server listing, tag "vps", French filter** (https://disboard.org/servers/tag/vps?fl=fr): the habitat of small French hébergeurs. Listings visible when this pack was made include a host advertising "des offres gratuites pour commencer" and one community project giving 100 percent free hosting from infrastructure in France, with registration through Discord. This is where French hosting trials are actually born; the agent reads this listing periodically and checks newly surfaced hébergeurs and their sites.
- **TeamKit** (https://www.teamkit.fr): a French community project that creates free dedicated game servers on machines in France, with no subscription and no card, requests handled through a short form read by humans. Small and games-specific, but a living example of the "serveur gratuit communautaire" pattern.
- **CHATONS directory** (https://chatons.org): the catalogue of France's alternative and associative hosting collective. Not a feed; a static map. Harvest the member host list once, then watch each member's site for new free services and trials.
