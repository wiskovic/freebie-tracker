# Guesses made while authoring this pack

The repository documentation does not answer every authoring question. This file records each point where I had to choose, what I chose, and why. Checks were made on 2026-11-14.

## Scope and structure

1. **Whether a pack may ship with no machine feeds.** `packs/SCHEMA.md` lists `feeds` as a block but does not require it to be non-empty, while its authoring rules say a pack deliberately carries no news-index layer. I chose `"feeds": []` because no Saudi freebie board with a stable RSS/JSON feed was found, and the alternative (Saudi tech-news RSS) would recreate the news layer the schema removed. The machine layer is instead the watched SDC events ground, which the runner can diff.
2. **The language list.** The schema requires a non-empty list of ISO 639-1 codes but does not say whether to list only official languages or every language the agent must read. I chose `["ar", "en"]` because the communities mix Arabic posts with English tool names and product pages, and the agent needs both to search and verify.
3. **The continent folder.** The prompt fixes `packs/asia/saudi-arabia/`, so no guess was needed for the location. The schema only constrains the spelling of the continent and country directory names.
4. **The third file in the pack folder.** The schema says a pack folder holds two files (`pack.json` and `README.md`). The task explicitly requires `GUESSES.md` in the same folder, so I added it. It is documentation only and is not read by the runner or linter.
5. **Cadence wording.** The schema asks for cadence "in words" but gives no vocabulary. I used `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly`, and `on wake, or weekly`, matching the style of the France pack.

## Sources and admission

6. **How to interpret the admission test for free education.** The schema says an entry must be "worth acting on, monitoring, and claiming", but the registry's category list includes `education` and `gov-programs`. I chose to admit free technical bootcamps, camps and courses from Saudi government or community providers, because they are the country's largest free allocations and are claimable through an application. I excluded general non-technical course channels and job boards.
7. **Which communities to admit.** I verified each candidate's live profile or channel before writing it down. I admitted `@TechDealsSA` (free tech deals), `@saudicamp_channel` (technical camps and challenges), and `@ksacourses24` (free remote technical courses, with noise noted). I excluded retail-only accounts (`@Lqth_SA`, `@Sales4KSA`, `@Ksaofffer`, `@todayoffers_sa`) because their posts are price tracking or consumer discounts rather than freebies, and I excluded `@msdevSaudi` because its visible posts stopped in 2017.
8. **Which hunting grounds to admit.** I admitted sources whose live pages offered a verifiable free or application-windowed opportunity: SDC events, Tuwaiq Academy, SDAIA Academy, the Adobe National Access Programme, and the Saudi Digital Academy. I excluded Saudi hosting providers whose "free" claims were only free SSL, free support or free migration on a paid plan.
9. **The Adobe offer as a hunting ground rather than a local pattern.** The schema treats `local_patterns` as structural facts and hunting grounds as places an agent reads. I put the Adobe/Firefly national offer in `hunting_grounds` because it is a specific provider page with an eligibility rule and an enrolment status that can change, not a permanent structural fact.
10. **The Saudi Digital Academy URL.** The academy platform (`sda.mcit.gov.sa`) timed out repeatedly and was not reachable from this environment. The MCIT service page for the same programme (`https://mcit.gov.sa/en/services/sda-bootcamps`) was reachable through a reader and states the cost as Free and the eligibility rules, so I used the MCIT page as the ground URL and recorded the platform instability in the ground's `how` text.
11. **Whether to mark communities as wake sources.** The schema allows `wake` on communities but the runner never pulls them, so a community cannot produce a diff that wakes the agent. I left communities at the default `false` and set `wake: true` only on the machine-watchable SDC events ground. The agent still reads the communities on every wake caused by that ground.
12. **Whether to add a `watch` block to the JavaScript-rendered grounds.** Tuwaiq Academy, SDAIA Academy and the Adobe page either render listings with JavaScript or do not expose stable listing links. Per the schema, I gave them no `watch` block and explained the limitation in their `how` text. Only the server-rendered SDC events page has a `watch` block.
13. **The SDC watch pattern.** The schema does not prescribe a pattern shape. I chose `events/[a-z0-9-]+$`, which matches the relative event links such as `/events/phishing-analysis` and does not match the `/events?tab=past` navigation link. The runner matches the pattern against each `href` separately, so this is safe.

## Local vocabulary

14. **The exact no-card phrasing.** The documentation does not give a Saudi equivalent of the France pack's "sans CB". I chose the three forms seen in live Arabic offer pages and the Adobe FAQ: `بدون بطاقة ائتمانية`, `بدون بطاقة`, and `بدون رسوم`. The first is the most precise; the other two catch shorter headlines and "no fees" wording.
15. **Arabic spelling variants.** Arabic free is written with and without the tanween (`مجاناً` and `مجانا`), and community posts use both. I kept both in `community_phrases` rather than normalising to one spelling, because search must match what people actually type.
16. **Which terms count as noise.** I chose retail and finance markers (`الشحن مجاني`, `توصيل مجاني`, `كوبون خصم`, `تقسيط`) as `negative_markers`. Free delivery and discount coupons are the dominant meaning of "free" in Saudi commerce search and would otherwise flood the agent leg.
17. **Telecom and bank promotions.** Saudi deal accounts constantly post telecom and bank perks, some of which are genuinely free. I did not make them a separate source: they surface through `@TechDealsSA`, and most require a Saudi ID or mobile number, so they belong in the safety-filter context rather than a dedicated ground.
18. **The "no dedicated Saudi freebie board" claim.** This is a best-effort conclusion from live search and source checks, not a fact any repository document supplies. I stated it in `about`, the README and `local_patterns` with the check date so a later author can revise it if a board appears.
19. **Density claims.** The schema requires dating density claims in a feed's `notes`. This pack has no feeds, so there are no feed-density notes. I dated the live-check statement in the README instead, and kept the descriptions qualitative rather than inventing percentages.
