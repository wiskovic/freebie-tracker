# Country pack: Saudi Arabia

A country pack is a filtered lens on one country, not a pile of that country's data. Every entry in this pack passes the same relevance test as the registry itself: it can surface a free tier, a trial, free credit, a giveaway code, or a community signal worth acting on, monitoring, and claiming. Technically, the runner validates this pack with the same id and format rules as the registry before running it.

Saudi Arabia's freebie surface is community-first. Worthwhile offers surface on Telegram deal and training channels long before any news coverage, and the largest free technical allocations are government training programmes rather than product free tiers. Most claimable offers are mobile-first and often require a Saudi mobile number, national ID or residency, so the no-card and no-fees markers matter as much as the local word for free. All sources below were checked live on 2026-11-14; the pack was matured the same day against a dedicated research record (community density sampled, the forum and Discord surfaces probed, a second watchable listing added, one noisy channel pruned). An independent claim-by-claim verification later that day pruned one further entry that no available route could re-verify, and corrected one community's cadence to match its real posting rhythm. A second pass the same evening re-attacked the claims the first pass could not settle: the ones a fresh route could prove were upgraded, and the ones no route could prove were reworded to say only what was actually checked.

## Local search terms (verified on live Saudi sources)

- Core nouns: "استضافة مجانية", "سيرفر مجاني", "خادم مجاني", "تجربة مجانية", "دورة مجانية", "معسكر مجاني", "أدوات مجانية"
- No-card markers: "بدون بطاقة ائتمانية", "بدون بطاقة", "بدون رسوم" (the highest-precision group for an owner's safety filter)
- Credit hunting: "رصيد مجاني", "كريدت مجاني", "أرصدة مجانية", "قسيمة مجانية"
- Community announcement phrases: "عروض مجانية", "مجاناً بالكامل", "مجانا بالكامل", "فرصة مجانية"
- Umbrella terms, only useful combined with technical nouns: "عروض", "خصومات", "كود خصم"
- Negative markers: "الشحن مجاني" (free shipping), "توصيل مجاني" (free delivery), "كوبون خصم" (discount coupon) and "تقسيط" (instalments) all mark retail or finance noise, not a freebie.

Strongest term formula: (مجاني) combined with (استضافة, سيرفر, خادم, دورة, معسكر), then checked against (بدون بطاقة ائتمانية, بدون بطاقة, بدون رسوم).

## Machine feeds

None. No Saudi freebie board with a stable RSS or JSON feed exists: this was checked when the pack was first authored and re-verified on 2026-11-14, when the largest Arabic hosting forum turned out to ban free offers by rule and to be read-only, and the legacy forums were dormant or carried no offers section. This pack deliberately carries no news-index layer: a news feed can only report what journalism covered, while freebie opportunities are grassroots events. The machine layer is the two watched listings under hunting grounds below (SDC events and Sabtiah events). The runner still validates and runs this pack; with no feed sources it diffs those watched grounds and reports their items.

## Agent-leg hunting grounds

- **Saudi Developers Community (SDC) events** (https://www.sdcsa.org/events): the events page of a Saudi developer community. It lists workshops, camps, hackathons and meetups, in person and online, with open registration; the site states no prices, so the agent verifies each event's cost on its own page and never assumes an event is free. The page is server-rendered and the runner diffs its event links.
- **Tuwaiq Academy open bootcamps** (https://tuwaiq.edu.sa/bootcamps?only_open=true): the open-registration listing of Saudi Arabia's national programming academy. Its programmes are government-funded and consistently described as free and fully supported, but the listing is JavaScript-rendered and its cards could not be sampled by any route available when this pack was verified, so the agent confirms each programme's cost on its own page before reporting it. The runner cannot diff it, so this ground belongs to the agent leg.
- **SDAIA Academy programmes and camps** (https://sdaia.gov.sa/ar/Sectors/BuildingCapacity/academy/bootcamps/Pages/default.aspx): the Saudi Data and AI Authority's training catalogue, covering AI, large language models and cloud systems. "التسجيل مغلق" means registration is closed; open cards lead to the current application. Programmes are government-funded, but eligibility and dates vary per cohort.
- **Adobe National Access Programme, Saudi Arabia** (https://www.adobe.com/sa_en/products/firefly/partner-models/humain.html): the national Adobe/Firefly access deal announced with HUMAIN and MCIT. Eligible Saudi citizens and residents aged 13 and over can register for 12 months of Firefly Standard plus Adobe Express Premium with 2,000 generative credits per month. Registration is an interest list only (re-checked 2026-11-14: enrolment not yet open); no payment method is required to register, and no charge applies unless a paid plan is chosen.
- **Saudi Digital Academy programmes and camps** (https://mcit.gov.sa/en/services/sda-bootcamps): the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology service page for the Saudi Digital Academy. The page states the service cost as Free and the eligibility as a Saudi applicant with English proficiency, national ID and admission selection. Registration runs through the academy platform (sda.mcit.gov.sa), which did not answer when re-checked on 2026-11-14, so confirm the current link on the MCIT page.
- **Sabtiah, Riyadh's AI Circle (Meetup)** (https://www.meetup.com/sabtiah/): a weekly two-hour AI builders' meetup in Riyadh, running since July 2025. The group page is server-rendered and carries its event links as plain hrefs, so the runner diffs them (the /events/ listing page does not expose event hrefs). Attendance is free community value rather than a claimable allocation; open each new event page to confirm the session topic and whether registration is needed.

## Communities

- **قناة المعسكرات والتحديات** (https://t.me/saudicamp_channel): a Telegram channel announcing technical bootcamps, challenges and courses from Saudi government and private providers. Posting comes in bursts around programme windows, with silent weeks between (checked 2026-11-14: the newest visible post was dated 2026-10-12), so a monthly read catches the bursts. Many posts carry their free terms in image flyers rather than text.

## Local patterns

- Saudi freebies surface first on Telegram deal and training channels; there is no dedicated Saudi freebie board with a stable machine feed (re-verified 2026-11-14: the largest Arabic hosting forum is read-only with posting frozen, still carrying its read-only notice that day, and its offers sections ban free offers by rule; the legacy forums are dormant or carry no offers section).
- The country's largest free technical allocations are government training programmes (Tuwaiq Academy, SDAIA Academy, Saudi Digital Academy), not product free tiers; they open and close on application windows.
- "بدون بطاقة ائتمانية" (no credit card) and "بدون رسوم" (no fees) are the precision markers for a trial that passes a no-card owner filter.
- Many claimable offers require a Saudi mobile number, national ID or residency; an owner without Saudi verification should treat them as blocked.
- Arabic deal search is dominated by retail noise: "الشحن مجاني" (free shipping), "توصيل مجاني" (free delivery), "كوبون خصم" (discount coupon) and "تقسيط" (instalments) mark commerce, not freebies.
